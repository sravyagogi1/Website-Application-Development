Peach Cobbler

An easy peach cobbler made with just 5 common ingredients you probably always have on hand. This simple dessert comes together in minutes. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream and whipped cream.

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 30 mins | Total Time: 50 mins | Servings: 12

Ingredients

  • ½ cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 cup self-rising flour
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 (16 ounce) cans sliced peaches in heavy syrup

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix flour, sugar, and milk until smooth.
  3. Pour melted butter into baking dish.
  4. Pour batter mixture over butter.
  5. Add peaches and syrup evenly over top.
  6. Bake 30–45 minutes until golden brown.
  7. Cool for 10 minutes before serving.

Recipe Tip

Do not substitute regular all-purpose flour for self-rising flour. Self-rising flour already contains baking powder and salt.

Nutrition Facts

Serving: Calories: 238 | Total Fat: 8g | Carbs: 39g | Sugar: 31g | Protein: 2g
