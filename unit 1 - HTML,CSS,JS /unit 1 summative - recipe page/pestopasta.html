Pesto Pasta

Pesto pasta is simple to make and full of flavor. This recipe is perfect for busy weeknights and comes together in just 15 minutes. Use any pasta and add as much pesto sauce as you like. Enjoy topped with plenty of Parmesan cheese.

Prep Time: 5 mins | Cook Time: 10 mins | Total Time: 15 mins | Servings: 8

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • 2 ½ tablespoons pesto (or more to taste)
  • Salt to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Fill a large pot with lightly salted water and bring to a rolling boil. Stir in pasta and return to a boil. Cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite (about 8–10 minutes). Drain and transfer to a large bowl.
  3. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a frying pan over medium-low heat. Add onion and cook until softened, about 3 minutes.
  4. Stir in pesto, salt, and pepper until warmed through.
  5. Add pesto mixture to the hot pasta. Stir in grated Parmesan cheese and toss well to coat.

Cook’s Note

If you have leftovers, they make a great cold pasta salad with freshly chopped tomatoes.

Nutrition Facts

Calories: 225 | Total Fat: 7g | Saturated Fat: 2g | Cholesterol: 44mg | Sodium: 71mg | Total Carbohydrate: 32g | Dietary Fiber: 3g | Sugars: 0g | Protein: 8g
