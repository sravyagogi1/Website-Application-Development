Sweet Potato Fries

These sweet potato fries with Italian seasoning and lemon pepper are a herby twist on traditional sweet potato fries. Crispy on the outside, tender on the inside, and packed with flavor!

Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 35 mins | Total Time: 50 mins | Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 sweet potatoes, cut into large French fries
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon lemon pepper
  • 1 pinch salt and pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Place sweet potatoes and water into a microwave-safe dish. Cook in microwave on full power for 5 minutes; drain.
  3. Add olive oil, Italian seasoning, lemon pepper, salt, and pepper; toss to coat.
  4. Arrange fries on a baking sheet in a single layer.
  5. Bake in preheated oven, turning once, until fries are crispy on the outside, about 30 minutes.

Nutrition Facts

Calories: 174 | Total Fat: 7g | Saturated Fat: 1g | Sodium: 130mg | Total Carbohydrate: 27g | Dietary Fiber: 4g | Total Sugars: 6g | Protein: 2g
