Lemon Pound Cake

This moist lemon pound cake is a blue-ribbon winner. Topped with a sweet and tangy lemon glaze, it’s rich, buttery, and full of bright lemon flavor. It always gets rave reviews!

Prep Time: 20 mins | Cook Time: 1 hr | Additional Time: 30 mins | Total Time: 1 hr 50 mins | Servings: 14

Ingredients

Lemon Pound Cake

  • 3 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 ½ cups white sugar
  • 1 ½ cups unsalted butter, softened
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 teaspoon lemon extract

Lemon Glaze

  • 2 cups confectioners' sugar
  • ¼ cup lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 tablespoon lemon zest

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour a fluted tube pan.
  2. Sift flour, salt, and baking soda together in a bowl.
  3. Beat sugar and butter with an electric mixer for about 10 minutes until light and fluffy.
  4. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
  5. Add the flour mixture in three batches alternating with buttermilk, beating well after each addition and ending with the flour mixture.
  6. Stir in lemon extract and pour batter into the prepared pan.
  7. Place cake in oven and immediately reduce temperature to 325°F (165°C). Bake 60–75 minutes, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
  8. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then invert onto a wire rack placed over parchment paper.
  9. To make the glaze, beat confectioners' sugar, lemon juice, butter, and lemon zest until smooth.
  10. Pour half the glaze over the warm cake. Let cool 20 minutes, then pour the remaining glaze over the cake.
